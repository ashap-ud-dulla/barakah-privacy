Barakah — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Barakah is a personal productivity app for daily deeds, prayers, tasks, money, debts, diet and fitness. It is built to be private first.

What we collect

Nothing. Barakah does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data.

  • Everything you enter (deeds, prayer records, tasks, money records, debts, diet and fitness logs such as foods, calories, weight, height, age and workouts, settings) is stored only on your device. The food and activity lists used for calories are built into the app, so looking up a food never goes online.
  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not request internet access, so your data cannot leave your device through the app.

Phone permissions and what they are for

Every permission is optional. Each one is asked for only when you turn on the feature that needs it.

PermissionWhyWhen it is asked

Fingerprint / face / screen lockUnlock the app when App lock is onOnly if you turn on App lock
  • Location. Your approximate location is used only on your device to work out prayer times. It is saved (rounded to about 1 km) with your settings. It is never sent anywhere. The app never asks for precise or background location.
  • Reminders. All reminders are off by default. If you turn one on, it is scheduled on your device only. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • App lock. Barakah uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock. Barakah never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Home-screen widget. The widget shows a small summary (next prayer, prayers logged, deeds done) on your home screen. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it. With App lock on, it shows only the next prayer time. You can remove the widget at any time.

Backups

  • Backup file (in the app). Settings → Data → Export backup makes one file with all your data. You choose where it goes (for example, your own cloud drive or a chat with yourself). You can protect it with a password. Barakah does not upload it anywhere by itself. Anyone who has the file (and the password, if you set one) can read it, so keep it safe.
  • Exported files. Money CSV files and meal or workout plan files are plain, readable files. They go only where you send them.
  • Android device backup. Your phone's own backup service (for example, Google backup or moving data to a new phone) may include Barakah's data. This is the normal Android behaviour, and it is controlled by your device settings and its provider, not by Barakah. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Your control over your data

  • You can delete all app data at any time in Settings → Data → Reset all data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Barakah is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
